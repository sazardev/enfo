<b>Every clock you need, in one calm app.</b>
Enfo began as a minimalist Pomodoro timer and grew into a clock and timer toolbox: focus, cook, train, breathe, sleep and keep track of time zones without juggling apps. Turn on only the tools you want and hop between them with one button.

<b>16 TOOLS</b>
• Pomodoro: focus and rest cycles, daily stats and streaks
• Clock: an always-on clock with 5 designs and a full-screen desk mode
• Timer: hour, minute and second wheels with saved presets
• Stopwatch: laps with best and slowest highlighted, down to hundredths
• Alarm: one-shot or repeating, snooze, rings full screen
• World clock: real time zones and daylight saving, plus a meeting planner
• Events: count the days to what matters, with yearly repeat
• Intervals: HIIT, Tabata and EMOM rounds
• Breathe: box, 4-7-8, coherent and custom patterns
• Tracker: time your activities and see a 7-day chart
• Kitchen: several named timers at once
• Sleep: a 90-minute cycle planner that sets your alarms
• Turns: a two-player clock and a speaker agenda
• Breaks: 20-20-20, stretch, water and posture reminders
• Ambient: white, pink and brown noise, rain, wind and ocean with a sleep timer
• Music: 14 lo-fi songs to focus to

The first six tools are on from the start. The other ten are one tap away in the Modes menu.

<b>63 CLOCK STYLES</b>
Watch your time as a ring, a wave of digits, a pizza, a lava lamp or a cookie jar. Pick from 63 flat timer visuals and 60 ready-made style and color combos, or mix your own.

<b>FULL-SCREEN CLOCK</b>
Turn any tool into a desk or nightstand clock: controls fade away, the screen stays awake, brightness can dim and the picture drifts slightly to protect your display.

<b>HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS</b>
Nine Android widgets: Clock, Analog clock, Pomodoro, Timer, Stopwatch, Alarms, World clock, Music and Focus. They match your theme, and Pomodoro and Timer use your chosen clock style.

<b>YOUR LOOK</b>
System, light or dark theme. 32 accent colors plus a custom color picker. Strictly flat design with a clean monospaced font. Choose which buttons appear in the bottom menu.

<b>STATS AND HISTORY</b>
Pomodoros, focus time, streaks and an activity history that also covers your timers, stopwatch, alarms and new tools.

<b>YOUR LANGUAGE</b>
English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

<b>PRIVATE BY DESIGN</b>
No account and no sign-up. Your history and settings are stored on your device, and you can clear or erase them any time. No ads and no tracking.

Enfo: less noise, more time.
