# Bundled music credits

The 14 Wikimedia Commons songs were re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis (~80 kbps average) to save space; no other changes were made. The 58 Open Lo-Fi songs get a deliberate lo-fi treatment instead (9.5 kHz low-pass, soft saturation, tape wow, a whisper of pink-noise hiss, mono 32 kHz, Vorbis q0), which also keeps them under a megabyte each. **CC0** songs are public-domain dedications (no credit needed; credited here anyway). **CC BY** songs require the credit below.

No attribution is required; credited here anyway. The selection favours the instrumental-leaning categories (focus and routines, ambient drift, chillhop, jazz lounge, Asian/zen, late night).
